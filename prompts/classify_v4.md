- docs: AadhaarAssist's documents can answer it. The documents are: UIDAI FAQs, Aadhaar (Enrolment and Update) Regulations (as amended), list of supporting documents, UIDAI circulars, Aadhaar Act 2016.

- off_topic: it is not about AadhaarAssist's subject at all.
